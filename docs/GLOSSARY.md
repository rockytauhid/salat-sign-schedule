# SalatSign — Terminology & Localization Glossary

This glossary documents standard Islamic and application terminology used across SalatSign in English (en-AU), Indonesian (id/in), and Arabic (ar).

---

## 1. Prayer Times

| Concept | English (en-AU) | Indonesian (id) | Arabic (ar) | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|-------|
| Dawn Prayer | Fajr | Subuh | الفجر | First daily obligatory prayer |
| Sunrise | Sunrise | Terbit (Syuruq) | الشروق | Astronomical sunrise (end of Fajr time) |
| Noon Prayer | Dhuhr | Zuhur | الظهر | Midday prayer |
| Friday Prayer | Jumu\'ah | Jumat | الجمعة | Congregational Friday prayer replacing Dhuhr |
| Afternoon Prayer | Asr | Ashr | العصر | Afternoon prayer |
| Sunset Prayer | Maghrib | Magrib | المغرب | Sunset prayer (also marks Islamic new day) |
| Night Prayer | Isha | Isya | العشاء | Night prayer |
| Call to Prayer | Adhan | Adhan | الأذان | Audible declaration calling worshippers to prayer |
| Second Call | Iqamah | Iqamah | الإقامة | Announcement that the congregational prayer is commencing |
