# Google Play Store Submission Guide — SalatSchedule (Android TV Home Prayer Times)

This document contains all metadata, store listings, graphic asset guidelines, and privacy policy for submitting **SalatSchedule** (`com.salatschedule.tv`) to the Google Play Store under the **Android TV** track.

---

## 1. Store Listing Metadata

- **Application ID:** `com.salatschedule.tv`
- **Category:** Lifestyle
- **Audio & Notifications:** Silent by default (import custom Adhan from USB/local storage) + optional 15-minute pre-prayer reminders

### App Title (Max 30 characters)
- **English:** `SalatSchedule: TV Prayer Times`

### Short Description (Max 80 characters)
- **English:** `Offline Islamic prayer times, Hijri calendar, reminders & Adhan for Android TV.`

### Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)
```text
SalatSchedule is a clean, offline-first Android TV prayer timetable designed for households and living rooms.

Key Features:

1. 100% Offline Astronomical Prayer Engine
Accurately calculates daily prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and Sunrise based on your city and preferred calculation method & madhab, with full daylight saving (DST) support and Australian city presets first.

2. Dual Gregorian & Hijri Calendar
Displays Gregorian and Hijri dates side by side with Maghrib rollover by default (or midnight rollover) and configurable day offsets (-2 to +2 days).

3. Live Countdown & D-Pad Browsing
• Next Prayer Spotlight: Prominent card with a live ticking countdown (HH:MM:SS) to the next prayer, including overnight countdowns across DST transitions.
• Remote Date Navigation: Browse yesterday, today, and upcoming dates effortlessly with Left/Right D-pad keys.

4. 15-Minute Pre-Prayer Reminders & Adhan Audio
• Heads-Up Reminders: Optional TV notification 15 minutes before each prayer time.
• Custom Adhan Playback: Ships silent by default. Import your preferred Adhan audio files (.mp3, .ogg, .wav) from a USB drive or local TV storage with per-prayer audio settings that play cleanly even while using other TV apps.

5. Designed for Android TV
10-foot high-contrast UI, overscan-safe margins, 12/24-hour clock format (12-hour default), and Dark, Light, or Auto Day/Night themes.
```

## 3. Privacy Policy Summary

**SalatSchedule** (`com.salatschedule.tv`) operates 100% offline with **zero** internet permission, analytics, telemetry, or personal data collection. All preferences and imported custom audio files remain strictly in app-private storage on the television.
